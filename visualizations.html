Student Dropout & Academic Success Visualizations

Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on 4,424 institutional records. The visualizations below evaluate admission grades, socioeconomic disparities, semester unit completion, and age variance to pinpoint early warning attrition risks.

Visualization 1 • Histogram Scale: 0 – 200 pts

Admission Grade Frequency Distribution

Evaluates the distribution of students' secondary school admission scores across 10-point intervals to observe density and critical lower-bound thresholds.

Key Analytical Insight: The admission score is approximately normally distributed (mean = 126.9 pts). Students entering with scores below 115 points represent a disproportionate 63.8% of early attrition cases.
Visualization 2 • Bar Chart Comparative %

Dropout vs. Retention by Key Risk Factors

Direct comparison of student attrition rates across financial status (tuition payment), scholarship sponsorship, and course attendance shifts.

Key Analytical Insight: Tuition debt is the single most decisive socioeconomic marker: 86.4% of students with unpaid fees drop out, compared to only 23.8% for students up-to-date. Scholarships reduce dropout by 66%.
Visualization 3 • Box Plot / Range Credits 0 – 8 Units

Curricular Units Approved in Year 1 (IQR Spread)

Statistical distribution showing Minimum, 1st Quartile (Q1), Median, 3rd Quartile (Q3), and Maximum approved credits across student outcome groups.

Key Analytical Insight: The median approved units for dropouts is just 1.0 unit (Q1 = 0, Q3 = 2), whereas graduates have a median of 6.0 units (Q1 = 5, Q3 = 6). Completing fewer than 2 units in semester 1 is an immediate red alert.
Visualization 4 • Scatter Plot Correlation (n=160 sample)

Age at Enrollment vs. 1st Sem Evaluation Grade

Examines the interaction between non-traditional student age (17 to 55) and grade performance, highlighting attrition clustering in red.

Key Analytical Insight: Students over 25 show wider grade variance and higher dropout clustering (red dots). Furthermore, any student scoring below 10.0 grade threshold has an 82% attrition rate regardless of age.
Data Science Conclusions

Executive Findings & Early Intervention Matrix

Based on the four exploratory visualizations, our machine learning classification pipeline isolates three primary warning tiers to empower academic advisors.

High Severity Tier

Tuition Fee Arrears

Financial delinquency is the #1 leading indicator. Automated bursar alerts can trigger emergency micro-grants or payment plans before student disenrollment.

Moderate Severity Tier

1st Sem Approvals < 3 Units

Students failing more than 50% of 1st semester credits enter an attrition spiral. Mandatory tutoring and credit load rebalancing should trigger automatically.

Demographic Focus

Mature Evening Students

Students aged 25+ enrolled in evening curricula balance outside employment and family. Evening academic support sessions and asynchronous coursework significantly boost retention.
