Featured Research Proposal

Educational Data Mining

Predicting Student Academic Performance & Attrition Risks

This research focuses on using classification models on higher education datasets to pinpoint key early warning indicators of student dropout. By evaluating academic performance, financial status, and demographic variables, the goal is to build an intervention framework for academic advisors.

Primary Question: How accurately can ML models predict dropout risk using early academic and financial metrics?
Target Variable: Student Status (Enrolled, Graduated, Dropped Out)
Key Predictors: First-term GPA, Tuition Standing, Credit Completion Ratio
