Educational Data Mining

Predicting Student Academic Performance & Attrition Risks

This research focuses on utilizing machine learning classification models on higher education institutional datasets to identify early-warning indicators of student dropout. By examining academic performance, financial variables, and demographic factors, the objective is to build an early intervention system for academic advisors to retain vulnerable students before semester completion.

1

Academic Trajectory

Curricular units enrolled vs. approved in 1st & 2nd semesters, cumulative GPAs, and admission score distributions.

2

Socioeconomic Factors

Tuition fee delinquency, scholarship sponsorship status, and family educational background as attrition catalysts.

3

Demographics & Attendance

Age at enrollment, day vs. evening classes, international student status, and gender disparities in completion rates.
